# Task 01 — Design Pack & Style Guide

**Spec link:** WJEC Unit 4, 2.4.2 Investigation · 2.4.3 Design
**Difficulty:** Core
**Time estimate:** 45–60 minutes (single lesson)
**Submit via:** Git repository or ZIP, containing a single Markdown or PDF design pack plus any supporting images

---

## Scenario

*Preseli Adventures* is a fictional outdoor activity centre in Pembrokeshire offering coasteering, kayaking, mountain biking and bunkhouse accommodation. Their current website is a single unstyled page of text and they have asked you to produce a **design pack** before any code is written — the same first step required for the real Unit 4 NEA.

## Requirements

Produce a one-page design pack containing:

1. **Two competitor screenshots or links** — find two existing outdoor-activity or leisure booking websites and note one thing each does well and one thing you would improve.
2. **A colour palette** — 4–6 hex colours (primary, secondary, background, text, at least one accent), each shown as a swatch with its hex code.
3. **A typographic scale** — chosen heading and body fonts (web-safe or a Google Font), with sizes for `h1`, `h2`, body text and small print.
4. **A low-fidelity wireframe** for the homepage — hand-drawn (photographed) or made in any tool — showing both a **desktop** and a **mobile** layout.
5. **A written rationale** (150–200 words) explaining *why* you made these choices for this specific audience (outdoor activity customers, likely booking on mobile, wide age range).

## Constraints

- At least **4 of your colour pairings** (text on background) must pass WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text/UI). Show your working — a contrast checker tool name and the ratio is enough.
- The mobile wireframe must show how the desktop navigation collapses (e.g. hamburger menu, stacked links) — don't just shrink the same layout.
- No code is required for this task — it is entirely a design/planning artefact.

## Success criteria

**Must**
- Include a colour palette with hex codes and at least 4 WCAG AA-checked pairings
- Include a desktop **and** a mobile wireframe of the homepage
- Include a written rationale linking choices back to the audience

**Should**
- Reference the two competitor sites with a specific strength/weakness each, not just a link
- Show a typographic scale with at least three distinct sizes

**Could**
- Include a short "content checklist" of what the homepage needs (hero image, activity list, booking CTA, opening hours, contact details)
- Suggest an icon or illustration style to accompany the palette

## How this links to the assessment objectives

- **AO2** (investigate, analyse, evaluate) — the competitor review and the written rationale are direct evidence of investigation and justified analysis, mirroring the investigation stage of the real NEA.
- **AO3** (plan, design, create, develop) — the palette, type scale and wireframes are the *design* stage of AO3, produced before any development work begins.

This task deliberately has **no AO1** content (no CSS knowledge is tested) — it is entirely investigation and design, matching Unit 4's own 0% AO1 weighting.
