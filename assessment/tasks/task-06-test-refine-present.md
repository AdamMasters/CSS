# Task 06 — Test, Refine & Justify

**Spec link:** WJEC Unit 4, 2.4.7 Testing, refinement & evaluation
**Difficulty:** Core, with a stretch extension
**Time estimate:** suitable for a double lesson, plus short homework to record the video
**Submit via:** Git repository or ZIP, containing a `test-plan.md`, your refined code, and a short video file or link (2–3 minutes)
**Justification focus task** — this is the task that explicitly requires you to justify your design/technical choices.

---

## Scenario

Before Preseli Adventures can launch, the site needs to be **tested against real success criteria, refined where it falls short, and presented back to the client** — mirroring the testing, refinement and presentation stage required at the end of the Unit 4 NEA.

## Requirements

1. **Test plan** — using the *must/should/could* success criteria from Tasks 02, 03 and 05, write a test log with at least **8 test cases** covering:
   - at least 2 accessibility checks (contrast, keyboard navigation)
   - at least 2 responsive checks (at 375px, 768px, 1280px)
   - at least 2 functional checks (form validation, navigation links)
   - at least 2 checks of your choice
   For each: what you tested, how, the result (pass/fail), and if it failed, what you changed.
2. **Refinement** — fix at least **3 issues** your testing found, and briefly note the before/after in your test log.
3. **A short video (2–3 minutes)** — screen-recorded, presenting your finished prototype and explaining, out loud:
   - one design decision and why you made it
   - one technical decision and why you made it
   - what you would improve with more time

## Constraints

- The test plan must reference your **actual success criteria** from earlier tasks, not generic statements (e.g. "checked contrast of `.hero` heading against background: 7.2:1, pass" rather than "checked colours")
- At least one recorded fix must be an **accessibility or responsive** fix, not purely cosmetic
- The video must be your own voice/narration talking over your own screen, 2–3 minutes — no slides required, just the working site

## Success criteria

**Must**
- Test log with at least 8 test cases, method and result for each
- At least 3 documented fixes made as a result of testing
- 2–3 minute video explaining one design decision, one technical decision, and one future improvement

**Should**
- Test cases explicitly reference must/should/could criteria from the relevant earlier task
- The video explanation gives a genuine reason ("I used a 3:1 gap because..."), not just a description of what was built

**Could — stretch extension**
- Carry out a short **peer usability test**: ask another student to complete a booking on your prototype without help, note where they hesitated or got stuck, and make one refinement as a result
- Reflect briefly on a decision you would make *differently* if starting again, not just what you'd add given more time

## How this links to the assessment objectives

- **AO2** (investigate, analyse, evaluate) — this task is the clearest, most concentrated AO2 evidence in the whole pack: structured testing, analysis of results, and a spoken justification of decisions.
- **AO3** (plan, design, create, develop) — the refinements you make in response to testing are still "develop" evidence, showing iteration rather than a single first attempt.
