# Mark Scheme — Task 06: Test, Refine & Justify

**Total: 16 marks** · AO2: 10 marks · AO3: 6 marks
**This is a formative classroom rubric — not the official WJEC NEA mark grid.**

## AO2 — Testing, analysis & justification (10 marks)

| Level | Marks | Evidence |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Developing | 1–4 | Fewer than 8 test cases, and/or results recorded without a clear method (e.g. "checked, fine" with no detail), and/or the video only describes what was built with no reasoning given |
| Secure | 5–7 | At least 8 test cases covering the required categories (accessibility, responsive, functional, own choice) with method and result recorded for each; video explains one design decision and one technical decision with genuine reasons |
| Exceptional | 8–10 | As Secure, plus test cases explicitly reference the student's own must/should/could criteria from earlier tasks, and the video's stated "future improvement" is specific and realistic, not generic ("I'd add more animations") |

## AO3 — Refinement (6 marks)

| Level | Marks | Evidence |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Developing | 1–2 | Fewer than 3 fixes made, or fixes made with no link back to a test result |
| Secure | 3–4 | At least 3 documented fixes, each clearly traceable to a specific failed test, including at least one accessibility or responsive fix |
| Exceptional | 5–6 | As Secure, plus evidence of a peer usability test with at least one refinement made as a direct result of observing a real user |

## What to look for quickly

- ✅ Each test case names the specific criterion tested (e.g. "hero heading contrast ≥ 4.5:1"), not a vague category
- ✅ At least one before/after comparison is visible (screenshot, code diff, or clear written description)
- ✅ The video is genuinely 2–3 minutes, narrated, and shows the actual working prototype
- ⚠️ Common gap: students describe fixes they *would* make rather than fixes they *did* make — only completed, verifiable refinements count for AO3 marks here
