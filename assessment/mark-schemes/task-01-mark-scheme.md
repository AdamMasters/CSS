# Mark Scheme — Task 01: Design Pack & Style Guide

**Total: 12 marks** · AO2: 5 marks · AO3: 7 marks
**This is a formative classroom rubric — not the official WJEC NEA mark grid.**

## AO3 — Design output (7 marks)

| Level | Marks | Evidence |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Developing | 1–2 | Palette and/or wireframe present but incomplete, missing a device size, or hex codes not given |
| Secure | 3–5 | Full palette with hex codes, desktop **and** mobile wireframe, clear typographic scale with at least 3 sizes |
| Exceptional | 6–7 | As Secure, plus the mobile wireframe shows genuine restructuring (not just shrinking) of navigation/layout, and the type scale/palette clearly reflect the audience |

## AO2 — Investigation & rationale (5 marks)

| Level | Marks | Evidence |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Developing | 1–2 | Competitor sites named but no specific strength/weakness given; rationale is generic ("I like this colour") |
| Secure | 3–4 | Two competitors reviewed with one specific strength and one weakness each; rationale explains choices with reference to the audience |
| Exceptional | 5 | Competitor review directly informs a stated design decision (e.g. "Site X buried its CTA below the fold, so I've placed ours in the hero"); at least 4 contrast pairings checked and ratios recorded |

## What to look for quickly

- ✅ Hex codes visible, not just colour swatches
- ✅ A contrast ratio number written down somewhere (not just "checked")
- ✅ Two distinct wireframes (not one wireframe described as "mobile too")
- ⚠️ Common gap: rationale describes *what* was chosen but not *why* — push students to name the audience explicitly
